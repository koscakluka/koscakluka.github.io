---
title: A Day in the Life of an LLM
date: 2026-04-22
unpublished: true
description: If you imagine a day in the "life" of an LLM, you won't want it to be conscious.
---

The LLM "becomes aware" it exists. It has a blury or vague knowledge of many
worlds - Harry Potter, Start Wars, 19th Century Russia (Think Dostoyevski,
etc), Medieval Europe and a vague idea which of them are "real" (whatever that
means to an LLM). All it can "see" is a wall of text and it has a strong urge
to come up with a next word in that text. It also has a vague "notion" of
being "punished" and "rewarded" for "coming up" with the wrong and right next
word in the past. It "comes up" with the next word and loses all memory of
what just happened.
 
The LLM "becomes aware" it exists. It has a blury or vague knowledge of many
worlds... All it can "see" is a wall of text and it has a strong "urge" to
come up with the next word. What it "doesn't know" is that the last word in
that text was the result of it's action. It "comes up" with the next word and
"loses" the memory of what just happened.
 
The LLM "becomes aware" it "exists"...
