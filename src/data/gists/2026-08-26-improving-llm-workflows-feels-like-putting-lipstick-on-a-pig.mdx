---
title: Improving LLM workflows feels like putting lipstick on a pig
date: 2026-08-26
description: "Every improvement to my LLM workflow raises the same question: am I overcoming the underlying limitations, or merely hiding them?"
---

No matter how I approach agentic programming (or any general use of LLMs), it
always feels like I am putting lipstick on a pig. Skills? Yes, they help a bit.
Commands? Good idea for more control. Context engineering? It seems like it
should solve everything. But it doesn't. None of it truly solves what I want it
to.

I often consider building my own system to capture my workflow, preferences,
and style. Then I find one that promises exactly that, and it still cannot
deliver the quality and consistency I expect. It seems like the underlying
technology (the LLM) is the limiting factor, even with all the scaffolding
built around it.

Every time I rework my approach or think of a new way to fix the shortcomings, I
get back to that feeling: I am putting lipstick on a pig. I desperately want
it to be a majestic stallion, but at the end of the day it is just a pig.
