---
title: Failure is the default state 
date: 2025-06-01
unpublished: true
---

As soon as we have a though that we want something, we failed at it. Now, where
is the logic in that?

The way I see it, failure is defined as a negative of success. While success
has a relatively clear definition (achieving a goal/intent), failure is absence
of success, that is, not reaching success.

That must mean that as soon as I wanted to be a rock star - I am a failed rock
star. There can be a variety of reasons - maybe I am not good enough to play
the guitar or sing, maybe I am too old, don't have enough time or money; maybe
I don't want it enough or maybe I just never started. The only thing that is
certain is that I never reached what I wanted - however vaguely that may have
been defined.

From a more positive perspective, if we are already failures there is very
little to fear. The only thing we can achieve by trying is success, and by
not achieving success we stay where we already are.
