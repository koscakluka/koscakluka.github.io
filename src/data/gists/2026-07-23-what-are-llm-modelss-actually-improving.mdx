I am unsure if the newest models are actually improving in coding or if they are
just more tuned to the harnesses they are used in and that is then presented
as "improvements".

there is no particular reason to increase the pricing, but rather that the price
is increased to gauge how much developers (and companies) are willing to pay for
the service.
