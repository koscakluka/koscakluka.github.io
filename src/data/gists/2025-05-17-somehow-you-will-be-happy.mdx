---
title: Somehow You Will Be Happy
date: 2025-05-17
unpublished: true
---

A couple of days ago I watched a [YouTube video]() by Ali Abdaal in which Joe
Hudson said that if "INSERT THE THING ABOUT BEING HOMELESS".

Although I did understand it from a logical perspective something wasn't truly
clicking for me. But over the days I tried to put it into practice for the
things I was doing, and I thing I found a formulation that works for me.

> I don't know how or why, but even if \<thing I am avoiding\> happens, you
> will end up being happy. Not just after it happens, even during it you will
> find a way to enjoy it. And I can't explain how, but you can choose to trust
> me on this.

Just reminding myself of this every time I notice I am tensing up, or I get
anxious or attached to a specific outcome or a situation, helps me relax and
move forward. Sometimes even with a smile.

This doesn't make me not care about the outcome, or not want things to go my
way, or stop doing things that will make the outcome I want more probable. It
just makes me less emotionally attached to the outcome.

Somehow I managed to convince myself that I can only be happy if I got exactly
the thing I want. And now, looking back, I see that everything I got was under
such tension that I barely got to enjoy it. And even if I didn't get what I
wanted, I sometimes still ended up enjoying either the process or even the
aftermath.
