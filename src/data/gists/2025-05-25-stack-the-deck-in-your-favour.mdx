I am not a big proponent of luck. Luck is, probably, a result of impossibility
to account for everthing. 

Looking at it that way, allows for building a deck of cards where the
possibility of drawing an unfavorable card is very low. I can build a deck of
cards where no matter if I draw willingly or I am forced to draw, I can be
confident it will turn out in my favour.

Building that type of deck might not be easy, but it makes playing the game a
lot easier. Some things that might be considered stacking the deck in my favour 
could be:
- Learning to learn
- Learning to converse well
- Being open to other people's viewpoints
- Taking care of health
- Investing in relationships

Sometimes I have heard people refer to this as meta-skills or meta-game. I just 
find the visualisation of the deck of cards to easier to understand.
