title: LLMs are idiot savants
date: 2025-10-23
unpublished: true
---

In his [recent interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY), Andrej
Karpathy taked about memory and intelligence in LLMs (among other things). One
interesting thing about the approach he takes is that LLMs could be categorized
by an outdated term "idiot savant".

For example, an LLM has an "idea" of how to play chess, but we have no idea if
that "idea" is at all realistic, i.e. if it actually matches the rules and
strategies of the game. Usually the LLMs are incredibly wrong when it comes to
facts - we call this hallucinations. This would be the "idiot" part.

However, once the LLM's context is filled with factually correct and even
instructional information, it starts outperforming average (and in some cases
even above-average) humans. This would be the "savant" part.

It seems LLMs have an impressive executional "ability", but as a consequence of
their training, their "perception" of the world is extremely inaccurate. If we
could strip the "perception" part out of LLMs, and find a way to inject accurate
perception with low-latency, the results could prove to be above-average or even
superhuman.

*\*LLMs are not humans therefore they cannot have an idea, or act, or perceive*
