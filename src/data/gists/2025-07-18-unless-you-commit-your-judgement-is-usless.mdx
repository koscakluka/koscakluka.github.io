So just a quick note on my thoughts, it will probably not be the final gist.

Unless you commit to make something work, when you say in the end that it does
or doesn't work it, your judgement is useless. To unpack it for a second. This
does not mean to be fanatical, but realistic about trying to get it something to
work.

You might have been suggested to approach a problem in a certain way, and 
instead of actually doing it like that (because you know better), you half-ass
it and it doesn't work. You just proved nothing, and using it as an argument
in any future reasoning or discussion is deceiving.
