Joe Hudson and Ali video is great and I keep coming back to it. It just got me thinking.
I also watched this other video (on being borded).

I kept going through this discussion with my girlfriend and we stumbled upon
the the idea that experience of the feeling is not the same as the experience
of avoidance of the feeling. I might go deeper into this some other time, but
what is important is how this connects to boredom.

If we are "feeling"  bored, that means boredom is a feeling. If it is a very
uncomfortable and strong feeling , and being "an attack" on our meaning and
purpose we can safely assume it is, we will probably tend to avoid it.

This make is probable that most people don't actually experience "feeling
bored", they mostly likely experience **avoiding** being bored. And, in my
experience, this feels even worse. Probably because it is denying the signal
from the body that you are on the wrong path and it would be benefical to
reconsider (check the video for more elaborate reasoning).
